Hi there, welcome to my personal website. My name is Arda Güler, and I am a PhD candidate and researcher at Goethe University Frankfurt. I am affiliated with the Chair of [Information Systems and Information Management](https://www.wiim.uni-frankfurt.de/en/team/arda-gueler), supervised by Prof. Oliver Hinz.

I obtained both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Mathematics, with a focus on Statistics, from the University of Bremen.

My research lies at the intersection of business, technology, causal inference, and analytical modeling. I primarily work with quantitative methods, combining empirical analysis with theoretical and analytical modeling approaches.

My current research projects include the application of causal machine learning in marketing, where I study and evaluate the effects of various marketing interventions on user behavior. In addition, I investigate how emerging technologies disrupt established markets through analytical modeling—for example, analyzing enforceable resale fees enabled by blockchain technology.

I am also involved in the Volkswagen Foundation–funded project “From Machine Learning to Machine Teaching”, where I apply machine learning methods and statistical techniques in medical contexts. This includes research on post-COVID conditions and community-acquired pneumonia.
